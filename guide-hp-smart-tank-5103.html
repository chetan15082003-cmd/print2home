HP Smart Tank 5103 Buying Guide 2026: Is It Right for You?

👤 Admin 📅 21 Oct, 2025 ⏱️ 8 min read 🖨️ HP Printers

The HP Smart Tank 5103 is a wireless all-in-one inkjet printer designed for homes and small offices that print frequently. Its refillable ink tank system dramatically reduces cost per page compared to traditional cartridge printers.

⭐ Our Rating: 8.5/10

The HP Smart Tank 5103 is an excellent choice for anyone who prints more than 100 pages per month. The refillable tank system saves significant money over time.

Pros and Cons

✅ Pros

  • Very low cost per page
  • Large ink tank capacity
  • Wireless printing support
  • HP Smart app compatible
  • Eco-friendly design
  • Great for high-volume printing

❌ Cons

  • Slower print speeds
  • Larger footprint than cartridge printers
  • Initial setup takes longer
  • No automatic document feeder

Who Should Buy This Printer?

  • Families who print frequently
  • Students with high print needs
  • Home offices on a budget
  • Anyone tired of buying expensive cartridges

Who Should NOT Buy This Printer?

  • Occasional printers who rarely print
  • Businesses needing fast laser speeds
  • Users needing professional photo quality

Available at major retailers including Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon, and manufacturer websites. Prices start around $149 - $179.
