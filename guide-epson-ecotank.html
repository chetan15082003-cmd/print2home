Epson EcoTank ET-2800 Buying Guide: Best Ink Tank Printer?

👤 Admin 📅 17 Oct, 2025 ⏱️ 8 min read 🖨️ Epson Printers

The Epson EcoTank ET-2800 replaces traditional ink cartridges with large refillable ink tanks, saving hundreds of dollars per year. It comes with enough ink for up to 2 years of printing right in the box.

⭐ Our Rating: 8.7/10

The Epson EcoTank ET-2800 is one of the best value printers available. The ink tank system pays for itself within months and delivers excellent quality.

Pros and Cons

✅ Pros

  • Included ink lasts up to 2 years
  • Extremely low cost per page
  • High resolution for photos
  • Wi-Fi Direct support
  • Eco-friendly refillable system
  • Massive ink capacity

❌ Cons

  • Slower print speeds
  • No display screen
  • No fax capability
  • Refilling ink can be messy

Who Should Buy This Printer?

  • Frequent home printers
  • Photo enthusiasts on a budget
  • Families tired of cartridge costs
  • Eco-conscious users

Who Should NOT Buy This Printer?

  • Occasional printers (high upfront cost)
  • Business users needing speed
  • Fax users
