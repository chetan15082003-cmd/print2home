HP Smart Tank 5101 Buying Guide 2026: Is It Right for You?

👤 Admin 📅 20 Oct, 2025 ⏱️ 8 min read 🖨️ HP Printers

The HP Smart Tank 5101 offers the same refillable tank technology as the 5103 but at a slightly lower price point. It is a reliable wireless all-in-one for homes that want affordable printing without sacrificing quality.

⭐ Our Rating: 8.2/10

The HP Smart Tank 5101 is a great value for frequent home printers. It delivers consistent quality at a very low running cost.

Pros and Cons

✅ Pros

  • Ultra low cost per page
  • Refillable ink tanks
  • Wireless printing
  • HP Smart app support
  • Compact design
  • Good scan quality

❌ Cons

  • No display screen
  • Slower than 5103
  • No Wi-Fi Direct
  • Limited paper tray capacity

Who Should Buy This Printer?

  • Budget-conscious families
  • Students printing assignments
  • Light to moderate home users
  • Eco-conscious consumers

Who Should NOT Buy This Printer?

  • Heavy business users
  • Users needing fast print speeds
  • People needing an ADF scanner
