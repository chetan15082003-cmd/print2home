Brother HL-L2350DW Review: Best Laser Printer for Home?

👤 Admin 📅 16 Oct, 2025 ⏱️ 8 min read 🖨️ Brother Printers

The Brother HL-L2350DW is a monochrome laser printer that delivers fast, reliable black-and-white printing at a low cost per page. It is perfect for high-volume document printing in home offices and small businesses.

⭐ Our Rating: 8.8/10

The Brother HL-L2350DW is the best monochrome laser printer for home use under $150. Fast, reliable, and very economical to run.

Pros and Cons

✅ Pros

  • Very fast print speeds (32 ppm)
  • Automatic duplex printing
  • Low toner cost
  • Reliable and durable
  • Large paper tray
  • Wi-Fi Direct support

❌ Cons

  • Black and white only
  • No scan or copy function
  • Heavier than inkjet printers
  • No mobile fax

Who Should Buy This Printer?

  • Home offices printing lots of documents
  • Students printing essays and reports
  • Small businesses on a budget
  • Anyone needing fast reliable printing

Who Should NOT Buy This Printer?

  • Users needing color printing
  • Photo printing needs
  • Users needing scan/copy functions
